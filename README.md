# Notewheel Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 4, 2026**

Notewheel is a note-taking app for iPad, made by Andrew Shentu. This policy explains what happens
to your information when you use it.

## The short version

Notewheel doesn't collect your data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and
it doesn't sell or share anything about you. Your notes stay on your iPad.

## What Notewheel keeps, and where

Everything you make in Notewheel is saved only on your iPad, in the app's own storage:

- your folders, notes, pages, drawings and text;
- the PDFs and pictures you add to your notes;
- your settings and the colors you've used.

Notewheel has no servers and doesn't send any of this over the internet. It doesn't sync your
notes with iCloud or any other service.

If you back up your iPad with iCloud Backup or to a computer, Notewheel's data is included in that
backup along with the rest of your iPad's data. Those backups belong to you and are handled by
Apple. Notewheel can't see or reach them.

## Permissions Notewheel may ask for

Notewheel only asks for a permission when you use a feature that needs it. You can change your
answer at any time in the iPad's Settings app, under Privacy & Security.

- **Camera:** used only when you choose Scan, to photograph pages, whiteboards or screens. The photo
  is processed on your iPad, to find the page's edges, and is kept only if you add it to a note.
- **Adding to your photo library:** used only when you choose to save exported pages as images to
  Photos. This permission lets Notewheel add pictures. It can't see the pictures already in your
  library.

Some things need no permission at all:

- **Choosing photos:** Notewheel uses Apple's photo picker, which hands over only the photos you
  pick. It never sees the rest of your library.
- **Choosing files:** Notewheel uses Apple's file picker, which gives it a copy of only the files you
  pick.
- **The clipboard:** Notewheel reads the clipboard only when you paste, and the iPad may ask you to
  allow it. It writes to the clipboard only when you copy.

If a photo or file you choose is stored in iCloud, your iPad may download it from your iCloud
first, as it would for any app. That's handled by iPadOS, not by Notewheel.

## Sharing and exporting

When you export or share a note, it goes only where you send it, through Apple's share sheet: an
app, a contact, Files, Photos or a printer. Once it's there, it's handled by whatever you sent it to.

## Deleting your data

Because everything is on your iPad, you're in control of it:

- **Deleting a note or a folder** removes it from Notewheel permanently. Files you imported for it,
  such as a PDF, are cleared from the app's storage about a week later. A file you uploaded in the
  note editor's From File list stays in that list, and in the app's storage, until you remove it
  from the list.
- **Updates:** before an update changes how Notewheel stores your notes, it keeps a safety copy of
  them on your iPad, in case something goes wrong. Only the three most recent copies are kept.
  They're left out of your iPad's backups and never leave your iPad, but a note you delete
  afterwards can remain in one until it's replaced.
- **Deleting the Notewheel app** deletes everything it stored on your iPad, safety copies included.

Copies may also remain in your own iPad backups until those backups are replaced or deleted. There
is no copy anywhere else, so there's nothing else to ask to be deleted.

## Crash reports and testing

Notewheel doesn't send crash reports or usage data by itself.

- If you've turned on **Share With App Developers** in the iPad's Settings (Privacy & Security →
  Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with the developer.
  Apple's privacy policy covers that.
- If you test Notewheel through **TestFlight**, Apple shares some information with the developer,
  as described in TestFlight's terms. This includes the email address you were invited with, crash
  reports, how the app is used (for example, when it's installed and opened), your device model and
  iPadOS version, and any feedback and screenshots you choose to send.

## Children

Notewheel doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If Notewheel ever changes how it handles your information, for example by adding iCloud sync or
in-app purchases, this policy will be updated here first, with a new effective date.
